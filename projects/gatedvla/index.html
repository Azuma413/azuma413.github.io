GatedVLA with Camera-Based Emergency Braking: AlpaSim E2E Closed Loop Challenge Technical Report

A driving VLA (Qwen-Drive-1.0-4B + flow-matching action expert) with a budgeted visual mask on the expert's cross-attention, plus camera-based emergency braking, for the NVIDIA AlpaSim E2E Closed Loop Challenge.

Abstract This report presents GatedVLA , our entry (Team HogeHoge) for the Physical AI AV (PAI AV) track of the NVIDIA AlpaSim E2E Closed Loop Challenge. The learned vision language action (VLA) policy pairs the driving pretrained Qwen Drive 1.0 4B vision language model with a layer wise flow matching action expert that outputs a 2.5 s horizon of acceleration and curvature controls, augmented with a soft visual mask applied to the expert's cross attention. Compared with an identically trained baseline, the masked policy reaches comparable overall open loop error while reducing error on left turns. In local closed loop evaluation over the same 150 scenes (two rollouts per scene), the visual mask raises the mean scene score from 0.479 to 0.517. Adding a rule based automatic emergency braking (AEB) module at deployment raises it further to 0.599 and cuts the mean number of scenes with at fault collisions from 34.0 to 11.0. Method Policy — two front camera frames and a fixed prompt go into the VLM; a 32 block π₀ style Diffusion Transformer reads each VLM layer through cross attention and predicts 25 steps of [acceleration, curvature] conditioned on ego history and a route target point 25 m ahead. Budgeted visual mask — a gating head scores each image token; a bisection solved bias fixes the mean retention at 0.2 by construction, and is added to the expert's cross attention logits. The gate is trained by self distillation so that it keeps the tokens carrying navigation intent.

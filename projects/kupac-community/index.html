KUPAC - Kyoto University Physical AI Community

Co-founded and organized KUPAC, a new student community for physical AI, and ran a hands-on workshop on imitation learning (ACT).

Overview I realized Kyoto University lacked a student community specializing in physical AI. To fill that gap, I worked with Assistant Professor Yagi and friends to start KUPAC. Key Activities As an organizer, I planned and ran a hands on workshop on pick and place using the ACT imitation learning model. Participants went through the entire pipeline: data collection, model training, and deployment. This activity is focused on contributing to our shared knowledge and building the community.
